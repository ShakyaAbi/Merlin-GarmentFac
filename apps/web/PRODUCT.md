# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal staff and operators who manage sales, inventory, purchases, and related business records in a browser app.

## Product Purpose

Help the team create, review, and manage business documents and operational data quickly and accurately.

## Brand Personality

Clear, practical, dependable.

## Anti-references

Overly decorative dashboards, cluttered admin panels, and navigation that exposes screens that are not part of the current workflow.

## Design Principles

- Keep primary workflows easy to find.
- Reduce distraction in the app shell and navigation.
- Make dense information scannable.
- Prefer direct, task-oriented labels.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard accessibility. Keep motion subtle and avoid relying on color alone for status.
